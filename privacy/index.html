Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Screenshot Scan (“Screenshot Scan”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Screenshot Scan mobile application (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

Screenshot Scan works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not have its own servers, and does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your screenshots, and the text read from them, never leave your iPhone. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

2. Photo Library Access

  • The App asks for access to your photo library so it can find your screenshots. It reads only your Screenshots album; your other photos, videos and albums are not read or analyzed.
  • iOS lets you grant full or limited access. With limited access the App works only with the screenshots you select, and new screenshots are not added automatically. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.
  • The App deletes screenshots from your photo library only when you choose them in the Cleanup feature and confirm the deletion in the iOS dialog.

3. Information Processed on Your Device

To organize your screenshots, the App processes the following locally on your device:

  • Text recognition. The text in each screenshot is read with on-device text recognition (Apple’s Vision framework, or Google ML Kit on devices where Vision is not used). The images and their text are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Categories and details. Based on the recognized text, the App assigns a category (for example shopping or travel) and extracts details such as prices, dates, addresses or links. This also happens entirely on your device.
  • Stored data. Small preview images, the recognized text, categories, the screenshots you save, your recent searches and your settings are stored in the App’s private storage on your device.

4. Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your device: reminders you set for a screenshot, and a weekly reminder on Monday mornings to review your screenshots. These are created by your device itself; no data is sent to us or to a push-notification service. You can turn notifications off in the App’s Settings or in iOS Settings at any time; turning them off in the App cancels all scheduled notifications.

5. Actions You Start

Some actions hand information to other apps or services, only when you tap them:

  • Open link opens a link found in a screenshot in your browser.
  • Share opens the iOS share sheet with the screenshot you selected; what happens next is handled by the app you share to.
  • Send feedback opens your mail app with our address; we receive only what you choose to send.
  • Rate the app uses Apple’s App Store rating prompt.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple — the App runs on iOS and uses Apple frameworks on your device (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • Google ML Kit — on devices where it is used for text recognition, recognition runs on your device and the content of your screenshots is not sent to Google. Like other ML Kit apps, the ML Kit library may send Google limited technical information, such as anonymous usage and performance metrics and device details, to keep the service working (ML Kit Terms, Google Privacy Policy).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your screenshots for advertising or to train any model.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data the App creates stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Data → Delete analysis data in the App, and all of it is removed when you delete the App. Deleting analysis data does not delete any screenshots from your photo library.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly hold any information about children.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the App’s private storage, protected by iOS. Because nothing is sent to our servers, there is no copy of your screenshots or their text outside your device. Keep your device protected with a passcode to keep this data safe.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since the App does not collect or store your data on our side, you can exercise these rights directly on your device as described above. For any question, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features that work differently. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest revision, and important changes will be announced in the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hasanbektasdev@gmail.com.
